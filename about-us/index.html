ABOUT US

Garant United Academy is an education platform of Garant Marine Group.

Our courses are delivered in partnership with Meridian Maritime Training Center in Batumi, Georgia. Garant United Academy provides the course catalog, enrollment and student support; Meridian conducts the training and issues the certificates.

INDUSTRY STANDARDS & COMPLIANCE

Course content is developed with reference to international maritime conventions and codes. Training is delivered by our partner, Meridian Maritime Training Center, under its certified quality management system.

ISO 9001

Designed in accordance with the requirements of ISO 9001.

Meridian Maritime Training Center's management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 by LRQA (approval no. ISO 9001 – 00032550).

LRQA

Certification body (formerly Lloyd's Register Quality Assurance) that approved Meridian's management system to ISO 9001:2015.

STCW

Meridian's certified scope: development and delivery of training programs for seafarers in accordance with STCW 78, as amended.

Maritime Transport Agency of Georgia

Meridian Maritime Training Center is recognized as a maritime training institution by LEPL Maritime Transport Agency of Georgia.

IMO

Course content references IMO conventions, codes and guidelines.

ISM Code

Safety management principles of the International Safety Management Code.

MARPOL

Pollution prevention requirements of MARPOL 73/78.

SOLAS

Safety of life at sea requirements of the SOLAS Convention.

Garant United Academy is an education platform; it does not itself issue STCW certificates and is not a classification society. Certificates are issued by the training provider, Meridian Maritime Training Center.
